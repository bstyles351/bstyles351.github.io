Pinball Machine

A handheld pinball machine, historically called a bagatelle.

Tools: CNC Mill, Laser Cutter, Sand Casting, Manual Lathe

Core Problem

Custom-made bagatelle with guidance from the Engineering Product Innovation Center.

Technical Contributions

  • Intro project through EPIC, designed to give familiarity with multiple different machining tools and processes.
  • Had design freedom for obstacles, theme, and internal layout.

Design Choices

I opted for a western theme immediatly drawn to the idea of a spur as the centerpiece of the design. The spur was made using a CNC machine, in retrospect the design I made was too thick for the force generated by the spring and ball. If I were to do this again the main changes I would make would be optimizing the spinner to reduce weight and inertia. I am very proud of the cacti I designed using CAD software, the ball fits well in the different arms and I was very thoughtful in ensuring that each of them could be reached. I engraved a muzzle blast below the spring which appears when the ball is launched, this aspect turned out even better than I had hoped adding real flair to the overall design. I engraved point markers in each slot on the bottom and in the cacti, these could have been larger and deeper cuts for more clarity. The brass handle was cut on the manual lathe allowing for sleek cuts and a clean finish. The aluminum frame was polished on a grinding wheel for a chrome-like finish. Overall this project was a success both as a creative outlet and a learning tool for complex machining.

Results

A fun game that continues to get compliments from friends and family who see it at my apartment.
