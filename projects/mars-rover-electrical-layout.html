Mars Rover Electrical Layout

Layout and wiring for mission-critical components of the rover. Submitted to URC 2026 finals.

Tools: OnShape, Soldering, Crimping, Heat Gun

Core Problem

Design and realize electrical layout for rover, ensuring stable power is supplied to all necessary components.

Technical Contributions

  • Created layout for battery, converters, fuse boxes, Jetson, and motors.
  • Used fuse boxes as rails to deliver proper voltage and currently while maintaining safety.
  • Optimized for wire size, minimal footprint, and easy reassembly
  • Created 3D printed mounts for converters, fuse boxes, and Jetson. Used metal rods for Jetson Mount.

Design Choices

Using the fuse boxes as power rails allowed for easy switching of components during the iterative design process. The modular approach to the electrical layout allowed us to be nimble and continuously improve and quickly integrate. The layout was also optimized to allow for future maintenance and troubleshooting leaving ample room for necessary tools. Each wire was cut, tinned, crimped, and heat wrapped to create strong electrical connections and proper current flow depending on the needs of the particular component. Each component was independently tested before integration to the final build to ensure reliability and eliminate potential issues.

Results

Submitted for University Rover Challenge 2026, missing competition by 2 points.

Judges praised our strong modular system design.
