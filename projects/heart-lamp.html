Heart Lamp

Heart Lamp for Lutron Lighting Design Competition.

Tools: OnShape, Soldering, Python

Core Problem

Each team must design and construct their coolest, most innovative lighting design project with a $50 budget.

We were judged on the following criteria:

  • Creativity.
  • Engineering Sophistication.
  • Aesthetic Appeal.

Technical Contributions

  • Designed heart-shaped shade out of 3-D printed clear PETg. Opted for a modular design connected with pegs to maximize overall dimension.
  • Created die shaped LED holders that allowed for evenly distributed light throughout entire shade.
  • Sourced materials and scavenged for replacements when supply chain issues arose.

Design Choices

We opted for clear PETg rather than resin so that it was easier to iterate on our design as the resin printers on campus have further restrictions. We opted for two buckpuck led drivers to help produce the requisite amount of current needed to drive multiple LEDs. This used up most of our budget so we took a risk and only bought one sesnor which ended up failing during the competition. We also unfortunately ran in to supply chain issues with the buck pucks and they did not arrive in time for competition. Thankfully I was able to borrow two from a generous lab on campus. This project challanged my design and programing skills having unique shapes and a challenging script using the noisy data from the sensor. Overall we were very pleased with our final result and the amount of lux produced!

Results

Judges praised our clean, safeguarded, and unique design while adhering to budget.
