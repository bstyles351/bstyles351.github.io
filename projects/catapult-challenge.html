Catapult Challenge

A catapult made for the 2025 SI Lab catapult challenge.

Tools: Chop Saw, Manual Mill, Tap and Dies, Drill

Core Problem

The competition guidelines stipulated:

  • Only materials found in the lab from the lab stock.
  • A 6-hour building period.
  • The catapult must fit in a 2 x 3 x 3' window.

Technical Contributions

  • Suggested unique crossbow design attempting to utilize previously never used heavy-duty springs.
  • Devised a pull bar to increase draw strength making use of human biometrics.
  • Used a bowline knot to properly secure bar to springs with strong connection.
  • Taught teammates how to drill pilot holes before screws ensuring limited wood supply didn't splinter.
  • Utilized manual mill to create guide ramp to help increase accuracy.

Design Choices

The crossbow system attempted to make use of the heavy-duty springs. We ran in to challanges in attaching the springs with the given tools and timeline. We took a risk with the springs that ultimately did not pay off with the result we wanted. We later found out that no group had succesffully used those springs before and the orginazers considered removing them from the supplied materials. I left feeling very proud of our design and I think the pull bar worked exceptionally well giving us a strong loading potential and a very repeatable operation.

Results

  • Launched a golf ball 25 yards.
  • Placed 5th overall.
